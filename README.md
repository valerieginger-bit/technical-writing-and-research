## 1. Water Quality in Port Arthur

**Project Type:** Environmental Research & Technical Report  
**Tool:** Microsoft Word  
**Role:** Researcher & Writer

Developed a research-based environmental analysis examining drinking-water quality and pollution pressures affecting Port Arthur, Texas.

The report combines municipal water-quality information with peer-reviewed environmental research to examine drinking-water standards, stormwater pollution, industrial contamination, ecosystem impacts, human-health considerations, and pollution-management strategies.

### Key Work Completed

- Analyzed municipal drinking-water quality information
- Interpreted reported contaminant concentrations and regulatory limits
- Researched stormwater, industrial, and non-point pollution sources
- Examined environmental and potential human-health impacts
- Integrated findings from peer-reviewed environmental studies
- Distinguished between point and non-point pollution sources
- Developed practical pollution-control and water-management recommendations
- Synthesized technical information into a structured report
- Applied in-text citations and developed a complete reference section

- Developed practical recommendations for teachers and administrators
- Applied reflective reasoning to educational practices
- Structured responses clearly around specific analytical questions
